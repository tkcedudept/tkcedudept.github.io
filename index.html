One Journey. Four Stages.

Different Ages. One Mission.

From preschool through high school, Sonland, JAMM, NOW and EPIC are four stages of one continuing journey of following Christ, each shaped for its age and all guided by the same biblical mission.

  1. Preschool

    Sonland

    Ages 3 – Kindergarten

    A warm, nurturing space where our youngest learn about Jesus through story, song, and play.

    Explore Sonland

  2. Elementary

    JAMM

    Grades 1 – 5

    A fun, Bible-centered environment where elementary students grow in their friendship with Jesus.

    Explore JAMM

  3. Middle School

    NOW

    Grades 6 – 8

    A place for middle schoolers to wrestle with real questions and grow in their walk with Christ.

    Explore NOW

  4. High School

    EPIC

    Grades 9 – 12

    A community where high schoolers dig into God's Word and grow as leaders in their faith.

    Explore EPIC

Our Ministries

Different Ministries. One Mission.

From our youngest children through high school, TKC EDU partners with families to help the next generation know Jesus, grow in faith, and live according to God's Word. Each ministry serves a unique purpose within our shared mission.

  • SUN

    Babies & Toddlers

    Explore SUN

  • Sonland

    Preschool · Ages 3 – Kindergarten

    A warm, nurturing space where our youngest learn about Jesus through story, song, and play.

    Explore Sonland

  • JAMM

    Elementary · Grades 1 – 5

    A fun, Bible-centered environment where elementary students grow in their friendship with Jesus.

    Explore JAMM

  • NOW

    Middle School · Grades 6 – 8

    A place for middle schoolers to wrestle with real questions and grow in their walk with Christ.

    Explore NOW

  • EPIC

    High School · Grades 9 – 12

    A community where high schoolers dig into God's Word and grow as leaders in their faith.

    Explore EPIC

  • Rapha

    Special Needs Ministry

    Serving individuals with special needs and their families.

    Explore Rapha

What Guides Us

Our Three Pillars

Three convictions from Scripture shape what we teach, how we lead, and what we pray for in every child and student.

  1. 01

    Born Again Believers

    “Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.”

    John 1:12 NIV
  2. 02

    Spiritual Leaders

    “Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity.”

    1 Timothy 4:12 NIV
  3. 03

    Lifelong Churchgoers

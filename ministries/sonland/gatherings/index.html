What we teach

Growing in God’s Word

  • Sunday Curriculum

    Gospel Light Preschool Sunday School Curriculum: an interactive, educational, and colorful way for children to discover how God’s Word connects to their daily lives.

  • Friday Curriculum

    The Jesus Storybook Bible by Sally Lloyd-Jones, helping children study the Bible as one unified story about Jesus.

  • Scripture Memory

    Memorizing Scripture is part of Sonland: children store up God’s Word in their hearts.

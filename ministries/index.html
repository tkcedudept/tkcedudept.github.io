Ministries

One Family. Every Generation.

TKC EDU partners with children, students, and families through six distinct ministries. From our youngest children through high school, and through specialized ministry opportunities, we share one mission of helping the next generation know Jesus and grow in faith.

The TKC EDU Family

Six Ministries. One Mission.

Each ministry serves a unique purpose, but together we form one Education Ministry committed to teaching God's Word, building relationships, and helping the next generation grow in Christ.

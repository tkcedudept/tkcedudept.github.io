About JAMM

What is JAMM?

JAMM is the elementary ministry of Thanksgiving Church, helping children discover who Jesus is, build meaningful friendships, and learn to live out their faith every day.

Who We Are

A Place to Grow.

JAMM is our children’s ministry designed to help kids grow in their faith in a fun, safe, and welcoming environment. Each week, children learn who Jesus is, discover their God-given identity, and practice what it looks like to follow Him in everyday life.

Our heart is to partner with families as kids build meaningful friendships, engage with God’s Word, and take steps toward a lifelong relationship with Jesus.

Our Vision

Eyes for Eternity.

Our vision is to help our children see beyond today and live with eternity in mind: knowing Jesus, understanding their identity in Him, and learning to follow Him daily.

“

So we fix our eyes not on what is seen, but on what is unseen, since what is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal.

2 Corinthians 4:18 NIV

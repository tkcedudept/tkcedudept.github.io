Good to know

  • About VBS

    Vacation Bible School (VBS) at Thanksgiving Church is a four day program for kids from Preschool through 5th grade that takes place every year in June. Hundreds of students and volunteers gather to experience the living God! Come join us for an exciting week of worship, bible study, drama, crafts, and more!

Questions

FAQ

How do I register?

Registration is online: use the Register button on this page.

Is registration online only?

Yes, we are only accepting registration online this year.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept online payments only. All transactions must be completed online.

How do I know if I submitted my online registration correctly?

Once you've submitted registration online, you will receive a confirmation email. Please make sure to use your correct email on the registration form. Check your SPAM or Junk mail if you don't find it right away. If you registered but didn't receive a confirmation email, please contact us at tkcjesusandme@gmail.com or sonland.contact@gmail.com.

Can I complete a student's registration form if I am not the child's parent or legal guardian?

No, only a parent or legal guardian of the child may complete the form.

What should my child bring to VBS?

Please have them wear their VBS t-shirt, name tag, and tote bag. Additional items can be their bible and a water bottle.

What time should my child be at church?

We invite you to come for dinner at our church at 5 pm. If not, classes line up at 5:30 pm.

Who can I contact with other questions?

JAMM (1st – 5th Grade): tkcjesusandme@gmail.com. Sonland (3 Year Old – Kindergarten): sonland.contact@gmail.com.

About TKC EDU

We Want to See Faith Last a Lifetime.

From preschool through high school, TKC EDU exists to help the next generation know Jesus Christ, grow in His Word, and faithfully follow Him.

Who We Are

One Journey. Four Stages.

TKC EDU is the Education Ministry of Thanksgiving Church. At its heart is one journey of discipleship in four age-based stages, Sonland, JAMM, NOW and EPIC, each shaped for a particular age and all held to one shared purpose.

A child can begin in Sonland at three and graduate from EPIC as a senior: taught by different leaders along the way, but guided by the same convictions from the first class to the last.

What Guides Us

Our Three Pillars

Three convictions from Scripture shape what we teach, how we lead, and what we pray for in every child and student.

01

Born Again Believers

“Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.”

John 1:12 NIV

Knowing Bible stories, behaving well and coming to church are good things, but none of them is our goal. We long for every child and student to know Jesus Christ personally and to receive Him by faith.

02

Spiritual Leaders

“Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity.”

1 Timothy 4:12 NIV

Leadership, as Paul describes it to Timothy, is not a position or a title. It is an example, set in speech, conduct, love, faith and purity. We want our students to set that example now: at home, at school and among their friends.

03

Lifelong Churchgoers

“And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another—and all the more as you see the Day approaching.”

Hebrews 10:24–25 NIV

Belonging to the church is more than attending it. We pray that our children and students will take their place in the body of Christ: gathering with other believers, encouraging one another, growing together, and staying faithfully connected to a local church long after they leave our ministries.
